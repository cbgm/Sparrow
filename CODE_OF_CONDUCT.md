# Code of Conduct

Sparrow's community is intended to be welcoming and respectful to everyone, regardless of background or experience.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- communicating respectfully and constructively;
- considering differing viewpoints and good-faith mistakes;
- focusing criticism on ideas and work, not people;
- respecting privacy and avoiding harassment or unwanted contact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
